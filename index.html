Privacy Policy

How Gully Score handles your data

In short: your scores, team rosters and photos stay on your phone. Gully Score works fully offline and has no server holding that information. The only thing the app sends is anonymous usage and crash data, which helps us fix problems, and you can switch it off in Settings.

Effective 28 October 2026 Applies to Gully Score for Android, version 1.0.0 (build 3) and later Contact k17114sn@gmail.com
The one thing to know

Every match, team, player and photo you add lives only on your own phone, in the app's local storage. Gully Score has no account system and no backend server, so there is nothing for us to collect, store on our side, or lose. The only information that leaves your device is the anonymous usage and crash data described in section 01, and you can turn that off at any time in Settings.

01Data we collect

Your match data stays on your phone. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, team names, player names, scores or photos.

From version 1.0.0 (build 3), Gully Score uses Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to learn how the app is used and to find and fix crashes. They collect:

  • Anonymous usage events — for example that a team or match was created, that scoring started or a match was completed, that a scorecard was viewed, that data was exported or imported, and which app language was chosen. These events never include team names, player names, scores or photos.
  • Crash reports — technical details of a crash, such as the error and where it happened in the app, the app version, Android version, device model, and the state of the device at the time.
  • Identifiers and general device information — a random installation identifier generated by Firebase (not your name, phone number or advertising ID), your device model, Android version, language setting, and an approximate region such as your country, which Google infers from your connection.

We use this only to understand how the app is used and to improve and fix it. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and Gully Score does not collect or use your Android advertising ID. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy terms. Earlier versions of the app (1.0.0 build 2 and before) did not send any of this.

The first time you open the app after this update, it shows a one-time notice about this, with a button to turn it off. You can also stop it at any time: open Settings and turn off Share usage and crash data.

This data is not linked to your name, phone number or any account, and we cannot tell which person a given installation belongs to. Google Analytics event data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days; after that Google deletes them automatically.

02Where your data lives

Everything you enter — team names, player rosters, toss results, ball-by-ball scores, match history, and any team or player photos you add — is written to a private database that lives inside the app's own storage area on your phone. It is not synced, uploaded, or shared automatically with anyone, including us.

If you uninstall Gully Score, this data is removed along with it, in the same way any other app's local data would be. Because of this, we strongly recommend using the export feature described below before uninstalling or switching phones.

03Permissions we ask for

Gully Score requests a small number of Android permissions, each tied to a feature you choose to use. We don't request anything we don't have an immediate, visible use for.

PermissionWhy we ask
Photos / media (Photo Picker) Only when you tap to set a team crest or player photo. You pick a single image through Android's own picker; the app never gets browsing access to your full gallery.
Internet and network state Only to send the anonymous usage and crash data described in section 01, when you have not turned it off. Gully Score does not need a connection to score matches.
Storage access (Save/open a file) Only when you use Export or Import in Settings, so you can choose exactly where a backup file is saved or read from.

04Exporting and backing up

Settings includes an Export data tool that writes your matches, teams, and stats to a single JSON file, and an Import data tool that reads one back in. Both use Android's standard file picker, so the file goes exactly where you tell it to — your device storage, an SD card, or wherever you choose to share it from there.

That file passes through your hands only. We never see it, store a copy of it, or have any way to access it once it leaves the app.

05Third parties and advertising

Gully Score does not show ads and does not use any advertising software. The only third-party services in the app are Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, described in section 01.

If ads are added in a future update, this policy will be updated first, and the update will describe exactly what the advertising partner can see — which, given the app's offline design, would still exclude your match data, rosters, and photos.

06Children's privacy

Gully Score is a general-audience scoring tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the only data the app sends is the anonymous usage and crash data described in section 01, which you can turn off in Settings. If you believe a child has used the app and you have concerns, you're welcome to contact us using the details below.

07Your choices

  • You can add, edit, or delete any team, player, or match at any time from inside the app — nothing requires our involvement.
  • You can turn off sending of usage and crash data at any time in Settings (Share usage and crash data). Turning it off stops further collection. Because this data is not linked to your identity, we cannot find or delete one person's data on request; it is deleted automatically after the retention periods described in section 01. If you have questions about data that was already sent, contact us at the address below.
  • You can export a full copy of your data whenever you like, and use it to move to a new phone.
  • You can revoke the photo or storage permission at any time from Android Settings; the app will simply ask again the next time you use the related feature.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes its local data from your device.

08Changes to this policy

If Gully Score's data practices change — for example, if a future version adds optional cloud sync, an ad network or new kinds of usage data — we'll update this page and the "Effective" date above before that change ships, not after.
